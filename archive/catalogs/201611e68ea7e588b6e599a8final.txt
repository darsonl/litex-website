Product Instruction

This switch is designed for the LiTex heating textile. It is an IC chip sealed within a silica gel shield protection
with a wide range input (B+/B-, 3.3V-12V/Max5A), thermal sensor (T1/T2), and an output part (P+/P-, for
connection with heating textile). By detection and on/off mechanisms, the controller can meintain textile at
target temperature. This module contains a safety switch that prevents heating textile from any sort of
overheating that would harm the product and user.
