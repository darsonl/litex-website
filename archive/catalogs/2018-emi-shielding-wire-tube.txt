EMI Shielding Wire Tube (Protect Sleeve Tube)

This functional fabric tubing is designed with the purpose of EMI (electro-magnetic
interference) shielding. Using our metal coiling technique, we have manufactured a novel
composite material of aramid (fiber glass) core covered with copper plating. The composite
material is then braided into a tube that may be used to encase any sort of cables in need
of EMI protection. This braided design allows the tube structure to expand, which means
the ability to accommodate a wider range of cables with different thicknesses, as well as
the bendability to wrap around all sorts of objects.

Applications

Cable casing for most sensitive instruments.

Advantages

· The strength of this fiber lies in its expandability. By compressing the length of the
   cable, the diameter may increase 1.5 to 4 times its diameter at taut state.

· Exceptional flexibility allows the fiber to bend or curve across any object.
· Incorporation of conductive yarn in the braided weave allows for superior EMI shielding.
· Three different diameter sizes ranging from 3-15 ø covers most of the cable size

   spectrum currently in the market.
· The soft textile nature of this material is a smart design that allows it to cover all forms

   of shape.
· The mesh structure formed from braiding reduces moisture and vapor buildup inside.
· Heat resistance: Can resist temperatures up to 600 degrees Celsius.
· RoHS certified eco-friendly.
· OEM / ODM available.
